# SEO Checklist

- Every indexable page has unique metadata through the Next.js Metadata API.
- Canonical URLs use `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` with a safe fallback.
- Sitemap, robots, manifest, Open Graph image route, breadcrumbs, FAQ JSON-LD, Organization JSON-LD, and SoftwareApplication JSON-LD are included.
- Sign-in, API, preview, debug, callback, and private application paths are excluded from crawling where appropriate.
- Pages use one clear H1 and logical section headings.
- Navigation, footer, solution, industry, resource, and blog content support internal linking.
- No fake reviews, ratings, clients, certifications, hidden text, or keyword stuffing are included.
